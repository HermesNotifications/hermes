# ADR NNNN: <short imperative title — the decision, not the problem>

**Status:** Proposed  
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Author:** <name>

---

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, business, operational? What constraints and goals
make this decision necessary now? State the problem neutrally, before the decision. Link
related ADRs (e.g. "supersedes [ADR 0003](0003-...)").

## Decision

The decision, stated in active voice ("We will …"). Be specific and unambiguous — this is
the part future readers will quote. If the decision selects between options, name the
chosen one here and leave the rejected ones for "Alternatives considered".

## Consequences

What becomes true once this is adopted — good, bad, and neutral. Include the new
obligations it creates (migrations, new dependencies, operational runbooks, follow-up
work). Be honest about the costs; an ADR that lists only upsides is not trustworthy.

## Alternatives considered

The other options that were on the table and why they were not chosen. Recording rejected
options is often the most valuable part of an ADR — it stops the same debate from being
re-litigated later.
